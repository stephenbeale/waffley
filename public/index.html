Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 February 2026

What data we collect

Waffley collects only what is needed to provide the service:

  • Account information — If you sign in with Google or Apple, we receive your display name and email address from that provider. Anonymous users have no personal information stored.
  • Learning progress — Scores, levels completed, streaks, mastery data, and game session history for each language and category you study.
  • Preferences — Your audio and display settings.

How we use your data

  • To save and sync your learning progress across devices.
  • To display your statistics and streaks.
  • To improve the app experience.

We do not sell your data, show advertising, or share data with third parties for marketing purposes.

Where your data is stored

Server-side data is stored in a Supabase-hosted PostgreSQL database (EU/US regions). Local data is stored in your browser's localStorage. All connections use HTTPS encryption.

Your rights

Under GDPR, CCPA, and similar regulations you have the right to:

  • Access — View all data we hold about you.
  • Portability — Export your data as a JSON file at any time.
  • Erasure — Permanently delete your account and all associated data.

You can exercise all of these rights from the Statistics screen using the "Export My Data" and "Delete All My Data" buttons.

Anonymous users

If you use Waffley without signing in, an anonymous session is created. Anonymous accounts have no email or name attached. You can delete an anonymous account the same way as any other account.

Data retention

Your data is retained for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, all server-side data is permanently removed immediately. Local browser data (localStorage) is also cleared at deletion time.

Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, contact us at privacy@waffley.app.
